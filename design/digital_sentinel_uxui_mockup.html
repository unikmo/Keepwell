Why the dashboard leads with coverage, not a "get help" button

The emergency action is deliberately secondary here (bottom-right, ember-colored, quiet) — the primary daily impression should be reassurance and daily-use value, not a standing reminder of a crisis that hasn't happened.

Why the dispatch screen shows ID + price before anything else

The research showed trust and price certainty beat discounts as the reason to stay in-app. The tech's identity and a hard price-cap promise are the first two things shown, ahead of the ETA countdown.

Why the vault explicitly disclaims estate documents

Scoped deliberately to everyday keys and codes, with a visible note ruling out wills/estate storage — keeps trust high without wandering into a regulatory category this product isn't built for.

Why the lockbox code sits above the contact list

It's the zero-cost, zero-dispatch resolution path — surfacing it first nudges members toward the outcome that's best for unit economics without ever saying so explicitly.
